Explainable AI in medical systems

From black box models to reliable clinical decision support

Today the patient is the model.

It is admitted with an impressive accuracy figure, examined with more than twenty explanation methods, stress-tested by three attackers, and discharged only with the papers a regulator expects.

Go to Part 1

Organised by the Center for Cyber Security Research (C2SR), University of North Dakota, Grand Forks, ND, USA. Prof. Dr. Utku Köse.

Every part follows the same three steps

First the idea, then the code, then everything on one bench.

  1. 1

    Explore

    A short page with live models and real patient data. Make a prediction first, then reveal what happens.

  2. 2

    Code

    The matching Colab notebook carries the full analysis. Run it, change a parameter, break it on purpose.

  3. 3

    Bring it together

    The XAI Lab puts every tool of the morning on one bench and closes with a debrief of the session.

Four parts and one lab

Times follow the official conference programme. Each card opens the page for that part; the notebook buttons open Colab directly.

Nothing to install

Every page runs its models in the browser, and nothing typed into a page leaves it. The notebooks open in Google Colab with a Google account; each one installs what it needs and downloads its data from the original public sources. Every dataset used today is real and openly available.

Participants who prefer not to sign in to Colab can follow every part on these pages and in the XAI Lab, and read the notebooks on GitHub.
